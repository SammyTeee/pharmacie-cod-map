Street mapping — v31

Four original vehicle props stagger the street: pub-side hatchback, delivery van, damaged car and a wreck at the closed end. Road barriers and service-lane gate define the boundaries; bollards, shallow planters and supply crates add frontage detail. The road centre, pavements and crossings retain routes around the props. Zombies comes first; PvP conversion is a later task.

Blender-only checkpoint. Gates, purchases, collision and zombie pursuit are not implemented or tested in BO3. Existing 8cm kerb steps remain; dropped crossings need further mapping. Vehicle placement is a gameplay adaptation, not photo-confirmed parking. Skeleton work is set aside.
