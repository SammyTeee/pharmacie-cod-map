Street and shopfront mapping — v32

Four original vehicle props stagger the street: pub-side hatchback, delivery van, damaged car and a wreck at the closed end. Road barriers and service-lane gate define the boundaries; bollards, shallow planters and supply crates add frontage detail. Wall-side benches, bins, shopfront lamps, plinths, vents, alarm boxes and florist displays add another layer. Drain grilles and flush service covers detail the ground. The road centre, pavements and crossings retain routes around the props. Zombies comes first; PvP conversion is a later task.

Blender-only checkpoint. Gates, purchases, collision and zombie pursuit are not implemented or tested in BO3. Solid approach ramps bridge the road-to-kerb rise at two crossings; game collision and stepping still need testing. Vehicle placement is a gameplay adaptation, not photo-confirmed parking. Skeleton work is set aside.
