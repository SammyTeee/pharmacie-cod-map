## Observed appearance

- Light paved footways run directly in front of shop bays with raised kerbs towards roadway.
- Some areas use brick/block paving, especially near brook-side bus shelter; others are plain slabs or patched surface.
- Manhole/service covers, gullies and threshold aprons interrupt pavement.
- Shop access steps/ramps and widened corners change levels/widths locally.
- Use actual depth/kerb geometry and decals only for surface markings; avoid photo people/shadows becoming permanent surface detail.
