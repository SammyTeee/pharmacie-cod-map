# Photo gallery

Collected reference photos for the Pharmacie Arms map. Each preview links to its source file in `references/pharmacie-syston/`. The AVIF source is shown using the existing JPEG preview; that preview is for browsing only and does not replace the original. Descriptions summarize visible details and suggested use for the blockout.

## Main room and bar

### Long room view

[![Long view through the main pub room](<../references/pharmacie-syston/pharmacie-arms-syston-1.jpg>)](<../references/pharmacie-syston/pharmacie-arms-syston-1.jpg>)

Establishes the long room and its front-to-back sightline. Use it to guide the broad layout, not as a flat wall texture.

### Room layout view

[![Interior view of the long main room](<../references/pharmacie-syston/images.jpg>)](<../references/pharmacie-syston/images.jpg>)

Shows tables, seating, and the central circulation area. Preserve a clear lane through the blockout.

### Main room toward bar and display wall

[![Main room with themed displays and bar area](<../references/pharmacie-syston/87288_9216d8f5.jpg>)](<../references/pharmacie-syston/87288_9216d8f5.jpg>)

Another wide room reference showing the decorated interior and the relationship between seating and the far end.

### Bar front

[![Straight-on view of the bar cabinet front](<../references/pharmacie-syston/bar%20front.jpg>)](<../references/pharmacie-syston/bar%20front.jpg>)

Clear, front-on reference for the dark drawer-front counter, brass-coloured pulls, taps, shelves, and lighting. Best used to model the bar; a cropped cabinet-face derivative could be considered later.

## Pharmacy displays and props

### Wide pharmacy display wall

[![Wide wall with vintage adverts and illuminated display cabinets](<../references/pharmacie-syston/53737352757_c7dacdf5d8_c.jpg>)](<../references/pharmacie-syston/53737352757_c7dacdf5d8_c.jpg>)

Shows the advert-covered wall, lit cabinets, medical objects, and nearby seating. Strong composition reference; the full image includes perspective and foreground objects, so crop carefully before considering it as a panel.

### Near-frontal feature-wall candidate

[![Preview of the near-frontal pharmacy feature wall](<../references/pharmacie-syston/_great-for-texture-preview.jpg>)](<../references/pharmacie-syston/great%20for%20texture.avif>)

JPEG preview of the original `great for texture.avif` (1973×1227), which shows vintage adverts, display cases, bottles, and medical objects. The original AVIF is linked here; retain it unchanged. It is the leading candidate for a single non-tiling feature-wall panel, subject to the BO3 material pipeline and image rights.

### Advert and optician display wall

[![Close view of pharmacy adverts and an optician display](<../references/pharmacie-syston/textures%20wall.jpg>)](<../references/pharmacie-syston/textures%20wall.jpg>)

Mostly head-on detail of the advert wallpaper and optical instruments. Useful for smaller panels and prop modelling; display objects are part of the image.

### Shelf and instrument detail

[![Angled detail of shelves, bottles, and medical objects](<../references/pharmacie-syston/aaaa.jpg>)](<../references/pharmacie-syston/aaaa.jpg>)

Close detail for bottles, shelves, instruments, and poster styling. Perspective and mixed lighting make it more useful as a modelling reference than as a large flat texture.
