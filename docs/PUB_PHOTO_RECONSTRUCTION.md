## How to interpret these notes

- **Observed:** visible object/form/finish in named image. **User-confirmed:** Sam's orientation/route correction. **Inferred:** paired views or position estimate. **Unknown:** not visible/established. Proposed gameplay dimensions belong in model manifests, not in observational notes.
- Interior left/right means standing at street entrance looking towards bar. Reverse-view photos towards entrance therefore reverse image-left/right. The upper roofline is described in the street document; ceiling tiles are not exterior roof geometry.
- Multiple photos are alternate dates/views/encodings. Repeated object sightings are evidence of the same object, not instructions to duplicate it. Select a documented state for banners, skeleton costume, furniture and lighting.
- Existing original estimate of 7m frontage, later 1.5x gameplay scale and subsequent widening are not photo-derived venue measurements. Near-1:1 visual proportions require preserving a calibrated reference model independently of intentional gameplay enlargement.
- The original council/evacuation plan and Sam's explicit staircase/exit corrections govern structure where photographs are incomplete. Photograph perspective cannot establish every back room, floor level or partition. Do not replace the skewed plan with a perfect rectangle just because a long-axis image looks straight.

## Room R01: overall ground-floor space and sightlines

Sources P03/P13/P14/P19/P20/P22/P10.

- Long relatively narrow principal room; standing near street entrance gives a long sightline towards counter/backbar screen. From bar end, front glazing is a bright broad opening with inset door and displays. Keep this visual axis legible.
- Warm honey/oak-brown plank floor runs through central aisle and unraised seating area. Individual planks have grain, knots/tonal variation and clear seams. Main direction appears along room length in P03/P19/P22; check against the chosen floor reference before UV assignment.
- Opposite side-wall decor is dense at eye height, with pale collage above a darker/cream lower wall band. This is not a minimalist undecorated brick pub interior.
- Suspended white/cream acoustic tile ceiling is low and nearly flat. Rectangular grid of pale/grey metal runners creates repeated square/rectangular panels. Slightly darker replacement panels, vents, recessed spot lamps and a black dome camera are visible.
- Brass/gold bell/cone pendants hang low enough to be visually distinct from ceiling grid, with pale/white lit interiors. Multiple pendants along room length; counter has additional glass shades.
- White ceiling-mounted projector near front/main aisle in P03/P19, with dark lens aperture and short mounting/bracket; a nearby cable/drop is visible. It is a compact overhead asset rather than a tabletop appliance.
- Small round/square pale wall speakers are mounted high on perimeter in wide views. Some black fixtures/vent grilles occur near ceiling. Exact counts/locations should be traced, not repeated at every grid intersection.
- Central route is narrower in crowded photos than required for co-op gameplay. Observed seating density and implemented widened clearance must be documented separately.
- Bar front is seen across the rear portion of principal room; service/toilet routes lie beyond/along side. Perspective alone does not establish true bar-to-rear-wall distance or the complete U-shaped counter footprint.
- Reflective glass/table tops and brass highlights create warm material contrast. Avoid colouring everything black because preview illumination is weak; cream lower walls, grey ceiling, amber floor and coloured adverts are real visual anchors.
